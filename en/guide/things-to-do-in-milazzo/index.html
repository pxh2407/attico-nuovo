Things to do in Milazzo

Castle, cape, beaches, history and food: the town to discover beyond the port for the Aeolian Islands.

Updated October 2026

The Castle and Citadel

It is the largest fortified citadel in Sicily: more than 7 hectares of history. Inside you can visit the Ancient Cathedral (1608) and the MuMa – Sea Museum. From the Attico Panoramico it is about a 10-minute walk.

Cape Milazzo and the Venus Pool

Cape Milazzo is a promontory about 8 km long reaching out into the Tyrrhenian Sea. At its very tip lies the Venus Pool (Piscina di Venere), a natural pool and one of the most spectacular spots in the area.

Beaches

Ponente Beach is a 5-minute walk from the apartment, and Croce di Mare beach is also just a few minutes away. A little further on is Baia del Tono.

History and culture

Milazzo was founded by the Greeks in 716 BC. Its waters saw Rome's first naval victory (260 BC), and in 1860 Garibaldi fought a battle here. Also worth a visit is the Sanctuary of San Francesco di Paola, the only one in Sicily dedicated to the saint.

Food and evenings

On the table: fresh fish, arancini, Sicilian granita and Etna wines. Near the Attico you will find restaurants such as La Campagnola, Adagio-Adagio and Macchianera, and the pedestrian area is the heart of the town's nightlife.

A walking itinerary from the Attico

  1. Morning: the Castle and Citadel (10 minutes on foot), with the Ancient Cathedral and the MuMa.
  2. Afternoon: the sea at Ponente Beach (5 minutes on foot).
  3. Evening: a stroll and dinner in the pedestrian area of the historic centre.

With an extra day: Cape Milazzo and the Venus Pool, or a trip to the Aeolian Islands from the port, a 15-minute walk away. For your journey: how to get to Milazzo.

Frequently asked questions

What can you see in Milazzo in one day?

The Castle and Citadel, a beach such as Ponente and an evening stroll in the pedestrian area: from the Attico Panoramico they are all within walking distance.

Where is the Venus Pool?

At the very tip of Cape Milazzo, the promontory about 8 km long reaching out into the Tyrrhenian Sea.

How far is the beach from the centre of Milazzo?

From the Attico Panoramico, in the historic centre, Ponente Beach is a 5-minute walk.
