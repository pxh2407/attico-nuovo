How to get to Milazzo

By plane, car or train: travel times, roads and what to know once you reach the historic centre.

Updated October 2026

By plane

The two reference airports are:

  • Catania Fontanarossa (CTA): about 1 hour 40 minutes by car, via the A18 and A20 motorways.
  • Palermo Falcone-Borsellino (PMO): about 2 hours 15 minutes by car, via the A20 motorway.

By car

Milazzo is reached via the A20 motorway. From Messina it takes about 35 minutes.

By train

Milazzo has a railway station on the Messina–Palermo line.

Once you arrive: parking and distances

Guests of the Attico Panoramico, at Via Cristoforo Colombo 7 in the historic centre, have a private garage included with remote-controlled access, and a lift in the building. There is an electric-vehicle charging station 200 metres away.

On foot from the apartment:

  • Conad supermarket: 300 metres
  • Ponente Beach: 5 minutes
  • 24/7 medical service (Guardia Medica): 5 minutes
  • Castle and Citadel: 10 minutes
  • Port for the Aeolian Islands: 15 minutes

To arrange your arrival, write to us directly on WhatsApp. And once you are here: things to do in Milazzo and the Aeolian Islands from Milazzo.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Milazzo from Catania and Palermo airports?

By car, about 1 hour 40 minutes from Catania Fontanarossa (CTA) and about 2 hours 15 minutes from Palermo (PMO).

Can you get to Milazzo by train?

Yes, Milazzo has a railway station on the Messina–Palermo line.

Where can I park in Milazzo?

Guests of the Attico Panoramico have a private garage included at no extra cost, with remote-controlled access.
